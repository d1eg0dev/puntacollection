The location

La Punta de Zicatela

The southern end of Zicatela: waves to surf, cafés, restaurants and the best sunsets in Puerto Escondido.

Where the houses are

All four sit on quiet streets in La Punta, between three hundred and five hundred and fifty metres from the beach. Close to everything, but off the noise of the main road. You walk to the cafés and restaurants, and you walk back at night.

The beach

La Punta is the southern end of Zicatela and has a character of its own: gentler waves than the big beach, surf schools on the sand, and a sunset that fills with people every day. It is where Puerto Escondido slows down.

Getting around

The streets in La Punta are unpaved, so people get around by taxi, scooter or quad. We pass you our trusted contacts when you arrive: airport transfers, scooter rental, tours and private chefs.
