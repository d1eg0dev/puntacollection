Before you book

Frequently asked questions

And if one is missing, message us on WhatsApp and we will answer in under an hour.

How far is the beach?

Between 300 and 550 metres depending on the house, all walkable. Aloha Punta is the closest; Punta Maeva and Punta Rosa are about a seven minute walk.

Is cleaning included?

Yes, daily cleaning at no extra cost. There is no cleaning fee at the end of the stay.

Can we host parties or events?

No. The houses are on quiet streets and we have promised the neighbours to keep them that way. For a small, quiet celebration, write to us before booking and we will look at it.

Can I bring a chef?

Yes, and we can also recommend one from the area. The kitchen is yours for the whole stay.

Are they good for children?

Yes. Do keep in mind that every house has a pool downstairs and a plunge pool on the rooftop, both unfenced, so small children need watching.

How do I get there from the airport?

Puerto Escondido airport is about twenty minutes away by car. We will pass you a trusted driver to pick you up; tell us your flight when you confirm the booking.

Can we book more than one house at once?

Yes, and it is what larger groups usually do. Write to us with your dates and how many you are, and we will put together the combination that fits best.

Why book direct instead of on Airbnb?

Because it works out better for both of us. Without the platform fee we can give you a better rate, you speak directly with the team that looks after the houses, and we sort out any change with nobody in between.
